A NEW, BIGGER FINDING
The fixed check also measures how much loudness information is present
inside the model, whether or not its loudness guess uses it. For kick, snare,
low tom and mid tom that ceiling is +0.14 to +0.38 in every arm, while the
actual guesses score -0.16 to +0.18. The information is getting in, and the
last step, which turns it into a guess, is not using it. That points at how
the guess is trained, not at the model's hearing. (The ceiling is measured
on the test songs themselves, so it may flatter a little. The gap is still
large.)
