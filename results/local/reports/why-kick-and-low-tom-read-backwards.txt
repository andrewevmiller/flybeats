WHY DID KICK AND LOW TOM COME OUT "BACKWARDS"?
Written 24 September 2026, after the overnight queue
==========================================================================

SHORT ANSWER
Mostly the way they were measured, not the model. The loudness check
scored every arm on a small, lopsided slice of the test songs and then
overstated how sure it was. Scored fairly, low tom is "can't tell" in every
arm. Kick is "can't tell" in three arms and only slightly backwards in A1.

WHAT WAS WRONG WITH THE CHECK
1. It only looked at the last 30% of the test songs, in file order.
   The test songs are listed drummer by drummer, so that slice is 29 of 36
   songs by one drummer (drummer 7). Only 14 songs in it have a low tom. So
   "backwards" really meant "backwards for drummer 7's playing". That is
   also why all four arms got the same verdict: they were all graded on the
   same slice.
2. It counted each hit two or three times, and treated songs as if they
   were thousands of separate samples. Hits within one song are alike, so
   the 95% ranges came out far too narrow. Resampled by song instead, the
   original slice's own range for low tom is [-0.47 to +0.23], which is
   plainly "can't tell".

The model's loudness guess is never fitted on those songs anyway, so there
was no reason to hold 70% of them out. The 30% cut only matters for the
side-by-side "ceiling" probes in the same check.

WHAT THAT CHANGES
- Kick and low tom are not a shared defect. The model's guesses carry no
  clear signal of how hard they were hit, which is "can't tell", not
  "backwards". (An earlier draft said the kick's loudness barely reaches the
  model at all. The fixed check shows it does [ceiling +0.18 for kick, +0.25
  for low tom]. The guess just doesn't use it. See 00-overview.txt.)
- Last night's verdicts should be reread. On this fairer scoring A2 (the
  arm that looked worst) is the only one that gets two drums right beyond
  the baseline (snare and mid tom) with nothing backwards. Both of those
  sit right at the edge of the range, so this is a weak hint, not a winner.
- A1 (loudness 5x) is the one arm with a drum truly backwards: the kick.

UPDATE: THE CHECK IS FIXED AND ALL FOUR ARMS WERE RECHECKED
scripts\probe_velocity.py now scores every test song, one row per hit, with
song-level 95% ranges, and its ceiling probes are cross-checked song by song
in shuffled groups. Rerun on all four arms, it reproduces the table above
exactly. The results are in runs\queue\SUMMARY_v2.txt, and the corrected
verdicts are in 00-overview.txt. The old probe files and SUMMARY.txt are kept
unchanged beside the new ones.

The test set is also half one drummer (60 of 124 songs are drummer 1).
Worth keeping in mind for every loudness number.
