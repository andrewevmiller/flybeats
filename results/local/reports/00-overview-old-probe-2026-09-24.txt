WERE KICK AND LOW TOM BACKWARDS ALL ALONG?  Yes. The baseline has them
backwards too, so none of the fixes caused it, and none of them fixed it.
Something all four share is behind it.

DID ANY FIX WORK?  No. None passes the project's bar, because no fix turned
a drum from wrong or can't-tell into right. Snare and crash were already
right in the baseline. The "snare improved" notes in the A1 and A3 reports
were measured against the old cloud baseline, and tonight's baseline cancels
them out.
  - A3 turned out almost identical to the baseline, round by round.
  - A2 is slightly worse: it lost the snare and its guesses are flatter.
  - A1 is the least bad: its guesses are the most varied, its timing is not
    hurt, and its loudness score was still climbing at the end. But its
    headline cloud result (both toms right) did NOT repeat here.

NEXT STEP (Andrew's call): the planned full-data GPU run (Phase B') has no
clear winner to carry. It may be worth first finding why kick and low tom
come out backwards, and scoring the models at the end of training rather
than at round 3-5. One run per arm: hints, not proof.
