WHAT THIS ARM CHANGED
The part of the model that guesses how hard each drum is hit used to pass its
answer through an S-shaped curve. The worry was that this curve makes it hard
for guesses to move away from "medium". This arm replaced the curve with a
straight line. Nothing else changed.

DID IT RUN CLEANLY?
Yes. All 12 training rounds finished (23:45 to 00:44, about 5 minutes a round),
then the loudness check ran in 35 seconds. No errors.

The copy of the model that gets saved and checked is the round with the best
timing score. Here that was round 3 of 12, and the loudness skill was still
improving after that (its rough loudness score rose from +0.06 at round 3 to
+0.13 at round 12). So this report reads an early version of the model.

BOTTOM LINE
Not a win. Removing the S-curve did not make the guesses more varied, and two
drums are confidently backwards, which fails the project's bar for a loudness
fix. This is one run, and it is scored on an early checkpoint, so treat it as
a strong hint rather than proof.
