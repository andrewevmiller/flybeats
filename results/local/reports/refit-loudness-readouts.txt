REFITTING THE LOUDNESS GUESS, ALL FOUR ARMS  (no retraining)
Written 24 September 2026, 09:40
==========================================================================

WHAT WAS DONE
Each of the four overnight models had its loudness guess worked out again
with one exact calculation on the training songs, instead of the slow
step-by-step learning that had left it stuck near its random start.
Nothing else in the models changed: same hearing, same timing, same
simple one-layer guess. It was then scored on the test songs with the
fixed check. The original models are kept unchanged for comparison.
Took about 4-5 minutes per arm on the CPU.

WHAT IT SHOWS
1. The information was there all along. With the same simple guess,
   properly fitted, every arm gets the snare and mid tom confidently right,
   and most get the low tom too. That is the drums that were "backwards" or
   "can't tell" last night.
2. Nothing is backwards in any arm any more. A1's backwards kick is gone.
3. Crash drops from "right" to "can't tell" everywhere. Its earlier "right"
   was mostly luck from where the guess happened to start, not a skill.
4. A2 (the straight-line output) comes out best: 4 drums right, and the
   strongest numbers on snare, both toms and open hat. Its fitted guess also
   tracked the training songs best. So A2 may genuinely help, now through
   the fitted guess rather than the trained one. That is still one run.
5. A1 and A3 add nothing over the baseline once the guess is fitted
   properly. A3 is the same as the baseline, which fits: both started from
   the same random settings and the rest of the model trained almost
   identically.

CAVEATS
- The guesses still vary less than real drummers do [spread 0.12-0.14 vs
  0.28]. That is expected for a guess that is only partly right, not a flaw.
- Timing is untouched: the refit only changes the loudness guess.
- This fixes the saved models after the fact. The standardisation run
  (finishing about 12:40) tests whether training itself can get there.

BOTTOM LINE
Loudness was not the model's hearing problem. It was the guess never
finishing learning. A quick refit gets three to four drums confidently
right with none backwards, and A2 is the best starting point.
