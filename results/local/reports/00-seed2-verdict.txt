Judged on the REFITTED loudness guess. The trained one barely leaves its
random start, so its verdicts mostly reflect luck, and the refit is what
makes runs comparable.

  Baseline, seed 1   snare, low tom, mid tom                                0.283
  Baseline, seed 2   snare, low tom, mid tom                                0.267
  A2, seed 1         snare, low tom, mid tom, open hat                      0.279

ANSWER: yes, narrowly, on both seeds.
- On both seeds A2 gets one drum right that its baseline could not, and
  nothing backwards, so it clears the project's bar twice.
- But the extra drum is a different one each time (open hat, then kick), and
  both sit close to the "can't tell" line. The shared three drums are the same
  for both recipes. A2 is also a bit stronger on them on average
  (low tom +0.37/+0.32 vs +0.22/+0.26). So: a modest, repeatable edge, not a
  different class of result.
- Timing: A2 is slightly lower on both seeds (by 0.004 and 0.007). That is
  small enough to call "about the same", though it points the same way twice.

PERSPECTIVE
The standardisation run (see arm-standardised-readouts.txt) moved things
