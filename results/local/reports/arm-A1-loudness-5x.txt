WHAT THIS ARM CHANGED
While training, the model is graded on two things at once: WHEN each drum is
hit (timing) and HOW HARD (loudness). The timing grade is about ten times
bigger than the loudness grade, so the worry was that the model barely
bothers learning loudness. This arm made the loudness grade count five times
as much. Nothing else changed.
