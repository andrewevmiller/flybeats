Is a working path enough? Yes. Moving the style push onto pC1 makes the dial WORKING: switching style changes 20% of hits, and the timing score drops only 0.003 (the limit was 0.01). It also brought more drums in and made the drive slider work.

Does training also need a reason? Not by this test. Silencing half the audio did not make style matter more. It halved the style effect (9%, just under the 10% bar), cost 0.007 of timing, and went back to mostly snare. Gaps are not worth keeping in this form.

These are cloud numbers. Timing scores compare only within this run, not with the laptop's (the laptop baseline also scored 0.2834, but that match is not evidence). A second seed of the pC1 arm would confirm it.
