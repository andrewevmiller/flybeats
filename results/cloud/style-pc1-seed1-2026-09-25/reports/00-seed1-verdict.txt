Does the effect hold on a second seed? Yes, in direction; not in size.
On both seeds the baseline's style dial does nothing (0%) and pC1's works.
On seed 1 it changes 11% of hits, about half of seed 0's 20%. That clears
the ~10% bar, but only just (a style never trained on: 9%). Timing held:
pC1 scored 0.018 above its own baseline on seed 1 (limit: at most 0.01 below).

What held on both seeds:
- The dial works only with the push on pC1 (0% vs 11-20%).
- Timing costs nothing measurable.
- The drive slider comes alive (6-11% of hits vs under 1%).
- More drums come in than the baseline plays.

What did not hold:
- The size: 20% on seed 0, 11% on seed 1. Treat "about 10-20%" as the
  range, not 20%.
- Seed 0's pC1 played at nearly the human's rate (8.5 hits/s). Seed 1's plays
  10.5, busier than its baseline.
- Tightness 1.5 no longer silencing the model was a seed-0 observation. On
  seed 1 the baseline was not silenced either (0.54x hits), so this run cannot
  test it.

Worth watching: on held-out kits pC1 lost a little more timing than its
baseline on both seeds (-0.017 vs -0.015, -0.023 vs -0.011). Small, and only
the seed-1 gap is outside noise, but it points the same way twice.

These are cloud numbers. Each seed's arms were trained on the same machine,
so compare within a seed. Seed 1 ran on a slower host (~415 s/epoch vs ~245 s),
which changes speed only, not results.
